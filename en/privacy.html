Developer and data controller

Cinearo is provided by Yusuf Emre Sencan. Contact cinearo@outlook.com for privacy requests and support. Your chosen content provider manages its own account service.

Free access

This version has no purchases, trial activation or payment restoration. It does not request purchase history from Apple. There are no ads or advertising tracking.

About this information

This page describes data use in the current iPhone version of Cinearo. Your content provider and Apple services also have their own privacy terms.

Your account details

If you choose to save a source, its name, server address, username and password are stored for this device in iOS Keychain. They are used in memory while connecting. A temporary session creates no new saved account. Cinearo has no separate user account or account sync server.

Connections to your source

Sign-in, catalog and guide requests go to your chosen provider; streams and artwork are requested from addresses supplied by that source. Those servers can see your IP address and requested content. Account details needed to connect are sent to your provider. Cinearo has no central stream relay server.

Search, guide and playback preferences

Catalog, category and program-guide requests go to your active provider. Search words are matched against the catalog on this device and are not saved or sent as a search query. Results stay in memory and are cleared when you change or leave the source. Account checks do not start a media stream. Preferred audio/subtitle language codes, including subtitles off, are saved on this device across source accounts; change them in Settings → Audio and subtitles. No track numbers are saved. The CarPlay background audio preference is also stored only on your device.

Connection security

Use HTTPS whenever possible. Traffic over permitted HTTP connections is unencrypted, which can expose account details and requests on the network. Cinearo does not verify a source’s trustworthiness or content rights.

Favorites and local information

Favorites and movie or episode progress for a saved account stay on this device, associated with that account. Records include content details, position, duration and watched status; they contain no password or stream address and are not sent to a Cinearo server. Viewing data for a temporary account is cleared when its session ends. Your language and last CarPlay connection status are also stored on the device.

Screen sharing

Screen sharing starts with iOS permission. Frames are processed temporarily in memory for the connected car display. No video file or screen archive is created or uploaded to a Cinearo server. This feature does not capture the camera or microphone. Notifications and private information visible on screen may also be shared.

What the iOS permission means

You can end sharing with Cinearo’s stop icon or the iOS sharing control. iOS permission does not authorize sharing other people’s private information. Ending sharing does not necessarily pause video in the source app.

Cache and diagnostics

iOS and the player may create temporary caches for streams and artwork. Cinearo includes no advertising or third-party analytics SDK. iOS has its own separate diagnostics settings.

Optional support correspondence

If you contact support, your sender name, email address and message are received through Microsoft Outlook to answer your request. Nothing is sent or attached automatically. Do not send provider passwords, payment details or private recordings. Correspondence is deleted within 12 months after closure, except where legal retention or an ongoing dispute requires the relevant record. Microsoft may process correspondence outside Türkiye. Sending an email is not consent to unrelated processing or every international transfer.

Manage your data

Remove a source in the account or saved sources section of Settings to delete its saved account, favorites and viewing data from Cinearo. You can also clear viewing records in Viewing activity. Signing out does not delete a saved account. These actions do not cancel your provider subscription or erase records held by the provider.

Your privacy rights

Under applicable Turkish data protection law, you may ask whether your data is processed, its purpose and its recipients; request correction, deletion or destruction under the legal conditions, and notification of those actions to recipients. You may object to adverse outcomes based solely on automated analysis and seek compensation for unlawful processing. Using Cinearo does not waive these rights or legal remedies.

Optional support email

Support messages are received by Emre Sencan through Microsoft Outlook to answer your request and troubleshoot the issue. Your sender name, email address, message and any attachment you deliberately add may be processed. Cinearo does not automatically attach logs, account details or screen images. Do not send passwords, payment details or sensitive personal information.

Purpose and legal basis

Support correspondence is used to respond to your request. Depending on the request, processing may be necessary to perform a contract or protect legal rights. The legitimate interest in answering voluntary support requests applies only where it does not override your fundamental rights. Data is limited to what the request requires; there is no marketing or customer profiling.

Retention and deletion

Support correspondence is to be deleted within 12 months after a request is closed, unless a specific legal retention obligation or an active dispute requires the relevant record. This is the owner-approved policy; deletion is handled manually and no automatic deletion rule is claimed to be configured. Device data can be removed using Cinearo’s account and viewing-history controls. Deleting it does not delete independent provider records or Apple-managed data.

Service providers and international handling

Optional support email is received through Microsoft Outlook. GitHub Pages hosts this website and original playback samples. These service providers may process connection information or correspondence outside Türkiye. Apple handles app distribution. This version has no in-app purchases. Your chosen content provider operates its own service. Cloudflare also serves the separate original sample source.

Requests concerning your information

Contact cinearo@outlook.com with the subject “Privacy request”. Describe your request without sending a password or an identity document. If identity verification is necessary, only proportionate information should be requested. The rights described above and your statutory routes to the competent authorities remain available. This contact channel does not prevent another lawful application method.

Tell us what happened.

Include your iOS version, Cinearo version and the steps that led to the issue. Do not send passwords, provider credentials, payment details or private screen images.
