Only while parked

Use video and screen sharing on a car display only while safely and legally parked. Do not watch video, select content or adjust settings while driving. Follow local traffic rules and the vehicle manufacturer’s instructions.

Keep the driver’s attention on the road

Moving images and notifications can distract a driver even without holding a phone. Stopping at traffic lights or in traffic is not safely parked use. Set up your display and connection before driving; keep vehicle controls and the view of the road clear.

Respect system limits

Do not bypass restrictions when your vehicle or iOS disables a feature. Cinearo does not remove vehicle or system safety controls.

Consider what's on your screen

Messages, notifications, password screens and other private information may be visible to people in the car during sharing. Check what is on screen before starting, and end sharing when you finish.

Temperature and battery

Video, screen sharing and charging can heat the phone. Do not leave it in direct sunlight or a hot vehicle. If iOS shows a temperature warning, stop playback and sharing and allow the device to cool. The system may limit charging or performance.

Mobile data and power use

Catalogs, artwork, guides and internet streams use your network connection. Mobile data and roaming charges depend on your carrier plan. Video and screen sharing can increase battery use. Ending sharing in Cinearo does not necessarily stop data use in the source app.

If the connection stops

Network, source, cable or system interruptions can stop playback. When the last CarPlay connection closes, Cinearo ends screen sharing and clears the last frame from memory; video in the source app may continue. Reconnect while parked. Resuming depends on the stream providing duration and seeking support.

Vehicle and local rules

A feature being available on screen does not mean every vehicle installation or use is lawful everywhere. Cinearo does not claim to detect vehicle motion or prevent every unsafe use.

Tell us what happened.

Include your iOS version, Cinearo version and the steps that led to the issue. Do not send passwords, provider credentials, payment details or private screen images.
