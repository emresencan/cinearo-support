Answers before you ask.

Does Cinearo provide channels or movies?

No. Cinearo is a player; it doesn't provide channels, movies, series, channel packages or provider accounts. Add your own source that you have the right to use to access real content. The demo catalog is for exploring the interface.

Where do I get a server, username and password?

Get them from a content provider you have the right to use. Cinearo doesn't create these details or recommend providers. Check your account's validity and usage terms with your provider.

Which accounts or playlist formats are supported?

This iPhone version connects using an Xtream Codes-compatible server, username and password. Importing M3U/M3U8 playlist files isn't available yet. Playing an HLS/M3U8 stream isn't the same feature as importing an M3U playlist.

Why can't I see any content?

Without a source, you can explore the interface using the demo catalog. If you've added a source, check that the correct account and category are open. A category returned empty by your provider also appears empty in Cinearo.

A stream won't open or keeps stopping. What should I do?

Try another item and, if possible, a different network. Check your account's expiry, the provider's simultaneous-connection limit and whether its server is reachable. If the issue occurs only in Cinearo, note your device, iOS and app versions and the steps that reproduce it. Don't share your password or a full stream address.

How do I check a connection problem?

Open Settings → Connection help, or choose Check connection from a playback error. The helper checks your active source account without starting another stream. Expiry and connection counts are shown only when the provider reports them. A successful account check does not guarantee every item will play.

Will Cinearo remember my audio and subtitle language?

Yes. Choose preferred languages in Settings → Audio and subtitles, or select a tagged language in Cinearo’s player. Preferences apply to future playback across source accounts. If your audio language is missing, the source default stays; if your subtitle language is missing, subtitles are off. Tracks without reliable language tags are selected for that playback only. Choose Source default to stop applying a saved preference.

Where can I see what is on a live channel?

Open a live channel’s details or tap the calendar icon in the live player. The program guide shows the current and upcoming programs when your provider supplies a usable schedule. Times use your device’s time zone. Missing guide data does not prevent channel playback.

Can I search all categories together?

Open Explore → Search library and enter at least two characters. Search covers channels, movies and series in your active source. Use the content type filter to narrow the results. If a content type could not be loaded, Cinearo tells you which one and offers a retry; it does not treat that type as an empty catalog.

Why are there black bars above, below or beside the video?

The video keeps its aspect ratio and the whole picture fits within the available area. Bars may remain if the video and screen have different aspect ratios. This avoids stretching people or cropping the picture's edges. Some black bars may be part of the video itself.

Can I resume a movie or episode?

In Cinearo's own player, supported sources remember your position after about 20 seconds. Movie details show Resume or Start over. Episode rows and movie posters have a thin progress line; completed items show a full line and a watched indicator. Saved accounts keep this on the device; temporary accounts keep it only for the session. Live TV and mirroring other apps are not tracked.

How do I delete positions and watched indicators?

In Settings → Viewing activity, swipe a record to remove it or clear all viewing data. This removes both positions and watched indicators. Removing a source account also removes its viewing data. Signing out does not delete data belonging to a saved account.

What happens when I switch to another app?

On CarPlay, playback pauses when you switch away unless Continue audio in the background is on in Settings → Audio and subtitles. Returning does not resume playback automatically. On iPhone, supported video can continue in the system mini player when Picture in Picture is allowed in iOS. Use its close button to pause or expand it to return. Formats that the system player cannot open continue in Cinearo without a mini window.

Why are audio or subtitle options missing?

Options depend on the tracks supplied by the stream and the player's support. Cinearo doesn't create audio or subtitle tracks that aren't present in the source.

Can I rewind live TV?

If the stream and player provide a seekable time range, you can use the timeline within that range. Not every live stream supports rewinding; the available range depends on your source.

What if CarPlay stays on Connecting?

Unlock your iPhone, check the system's CarPlay prompts and open Cinearo on the car display. For a wired connection, check the cable and USB port and reconnect. For a wireless connection, follow your car's pairing instructions. Video support can vary between cars and connections.

Why doesn't YouTube's timeline appear when mirroring?

Mirroring sends your phone's picture to the car display; it doesn't send CarPlay touches to YouTube. Use your phone for YouTube's play/pause and timeline controls. The square icon in Cinearo ends screen sharing, but doesn't pause the video.

Why is video black in some apps?

The service may prevent protected content from being captured or shown on another display. Cinearo doesn't bypass DRM or system restrictions and doesn't guarantee that every app can be shared.

Can I use it with a Samsung TV?

If your TV supports AirPlay, you can use iOS Screen Mirroring when your iPhone and TV are on the same Wi-Fi network. This iPhone app doesn't install a separate Samsung/Tizen app on the TV. AirPlay support depends on your TV model.

Where are my sign-in details stored?

If you choose to save them, they're kept in iOS Keychain for this device only. A temporary session doesn't create a new saved account. Account details are sent to your chosen provider to connect. HTTP network traffic isn't encrypted.

How do I delete a saved account and its favorites?

Remove the account through Provider and account or Saved sources in Settings. Its favorites are also deleted. Signing out doesn't delete the saved account. Removing it doesn't cancel your provider subscription.

Does screen sharing create a video recording?

No. The system permission prompt may mention recording/sharing, but Cinearo uses the feature for live display. Images are processed temporarily in memory. No video file, screen archive or viewing history of mirrored apps is created. Remember that notifications may also be visible during sharing.

Tell us what happened.

Include your iOS version, Cinearo version and the steps that led to the issue. Do not send passwords, provider credentials, payment details or private screen images.
