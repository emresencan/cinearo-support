Free access

All player and screen-sharing features in this version are free. There is no Premium purchase, trial limit or automatic charge. Cinearo does not provide TV channels, films or content subscriptions. Compatibility, content rights and system safety restrictions still apply.

Cinearo is a player

Cinearo is an independent media player for compatible sources you add yourself. It does not provide channels, movies, series, stream subscriptions or provider accounts. The demo catalog only introduces the interface.

Content and the right to use it

Only add sources you have the right to access and use. Follow your source’s terms, copyright requirements and regional restrictions. A working link does not grant permission to use content or share it with others.

Third-party services

Catalogs, artwork, stream addresses and account status come from your source. Content availability and quality depend on the provider and your connection. Contact your provider about its accounts or subscriptions.

Protection and sharing

Cinearo does not remove DRM, access controls or system security restrictions. Screen sharing does not grant permission to record or redistribute third-party content. Follow the service’s sharing terms.

Compatible devices and protected content

Playback and casting depend on the media format, device, iOS version and connected display. Screen sharing requires iOS 27 or later. AirPlay and car video require a compatible TV or vehicle; a CarPlay connection alone does not provide video support. Protected content may appear black or have unavailable audio.

If the connection stops

Network, source, cable or system interruptions can stop playback. When the last CarPlay connection closes, Cinearo ends screen sharing and clears the last frame from memory; video in the source app may continue. Reconnect while parked. Resuming depends on the stream providing duration and seeking support.

Mobile data and power use

Catalogs, artwork, guides and internet streams use your network connection. Mobile data and roaming charges depend on your carrier plan. Video and screen sharing can increase battery use. Ending sharing in Cinearo does not necessarily stop data use in the source app.

Your rights and responsibilities

This information does not limit mandatory rights under consumer or data protection law, or access to competent authorities and courts. Obligations and liabilities that cannot lawfully be excluded remain in effect.

An independent app

Cinearo is not an official product of Apple, Samsung, VideoLAN or content providers. It does not imply their endorsement or partnership. Trademarks and content belong to their respective owners.
